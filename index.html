Olatunde Salami

I’m a geoscientist and software engineer. I run Tareony, a software studio for high-stakes decisions in healthcare, finance, and energy: compliance operations for home health agencies and fintechs, and a deal screen for oil and gas acquisitions.

On my own time I build in the open, from a public knowledge graph of Nigeria’s government to a plugin for watching AI coding agents browse. The thread through all of it is evidence: data you can trace to the source, with gaps shown instead of guessed.

Projects

Tareony

My software studio, building operational software where every decision carries its evidence: compliance work that becomes an audit-ready record, sign-offs an auditor can verify, and oil and gas deals tested against the offset wells producing next door.

herdr-browser

An open-source Herdr plugin that shows the browser AI coding agents drive through Chrome DevTools or Playwright MCP in a terminal pane, live, so you can watch Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Droid or Antigravity work and take over the same page.

Who Runs Naija

A public knowledge graph of Nigeria’s government: ministries, seats, officeholders, and the sourced edges that connect who appoints, confirms, elects, and oversees whom.

Data Center Site Selection Engine

A spatial clustering tool for 100MW+ hyperscale development, optimizing heavy energy loads by intersecting national transmission grid capacity with USGS groundwater data.
